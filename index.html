Yard Wise Privacy Policy

Yard Wise | Effective Date: September 10, 2026 | Last Updated: September 10, 2026

1 Who We Are and the Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ZHIZIYUANSHUI, located at Room 526, 5th Floor, Building 1, No. 17 Xijing Road, Shijingshan District, Beijing, China ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information in connection with the Yard Wise iOS application. Our privacy contact is zhiziyunketang@163.com.

This Policy covers the current version of Yard Wise and support communications you send to us. Yard Wise does not require an account. Photograph analysis is performed on your device by an automated algorithm and does not use an external or cloud analysis service.

2 Summary of Our Data Practices

Yard Wise is designed so the photograph selected for analysis remains on your device. We do not receive or store that photograph, analysis data, report content, voice notes, or location context through the app. We do not use advertising or tracking technologies, sell personal information, or share personal information for targeted advertising.

Information leaves your device only when you deliberately send a support message or choose an Apple or third-party destination to save, print, or share a PDF report. Those actions and their recipients are described below.

3 Photographs Analysis Results and Reports

When you take a photograph or select one from your photo library, Yard Wise processes the image on your device to produce an informational analysis. The analysis copy is deleted immediately after processing is complete. We do not upload the photograph or analysis output to our servers or to a third-party analysis provider.

The app may temporarily hold the selected image and intermediate data in device memory while it performs the requested analysis and builds the PDF. The resulting analysis and PDF remain under your control on the device unless you save or share them. A source image already in your photo library remains there until you delete it using iOS controls.

4 Permissions and Information Processed on Your Device

Yard Wise requests a permission only when needed for a related function. You can deny or later withdraw a permission in iOS Settings. Information processed under the permissions below remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

  • Camera: to capture a yard photograph that you choose to analyze.
  • Photo Library Read Access: to let you select an existing photograph for analysis.
  • Photo Library Add Access: to save a rendered image from a report when you choose that option.
  • Location: to include location context in a report when you choose that function. Yard Wise does not use location for tracking or advertising.
  • Microphone: to capture an optional voice note or dictation only when you initiate that function. Yard Wise does not listen in the background.
  • Notifications: to provide local report-status or reminder notifications. Yard Wise does not send a device push token to our server for these local notifications.

Yard Wise does not access contacts, calendars, Apple Health or HealthKit data, Bluetooth data, or motion and fitness data. The iOS file picker, print panel, and share sheet allow you to choose a file location, printer, recipient, or destination without giving Yard Wise unrestricted access to those services.

5 No Account Analytics Advertising or Tracking

Yard Wise does not require registration or sign-in and does not create a server-side user profile. The current version does not use third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting software development kits. We do not collect IP addresses, device or installation identifiers, advertising identifiers, app-use events, or diagnostic reports through the app.

Yard Wise does not display advertising, track you across apps or websites, sell personal information, share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or disclose personal information to data brokers. The current version does not offer in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paid reports and therefore does not receive purchase history or payment information.

6 Saving Printing and Sharing You Choose

When you choose to save, print, or share a PDF report, the report and any photograph, location context, note, or other information included in it are sent to the Apple feature or destination you select. Yard Wise does not automatically publish reports or select a recipient for you.

Apple, an email provider, a printer service, cloud-storage provider, messaging app, or another recipient may process and retain the copy under its own privacy policy. Review the report and destination before sharing. We do not control or remotely delete copies held by a destination you select.

7 Support Communications

If you contact us at zhiziyunketang@163.com, we receive the email address or contact information you use, your message, and any attachment you choose to send. We use this information to respond to and resolve your request, protect the support channel, and maintain a record of the resolution. Do not send photographs, reports, precise location, voice recordings, or other sensitive material unless it is necessary for your request.

Our email service provider processes support messages to transmit and store email on our behalf. Support messages may be processed in locations where that provider operates. Where applicable law requires a transfer safeguard, we use the legally required mechanism or obtain consent where appropriate.

8 Purposes and Legal Bases

On-device processing occurs to perform the function you request. Permission-dependent processing is based on your choice and the permission you grant through iOS. You may withdraw that permission in iOS Settings, which stops future access within the withdrawn scope.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis for support communications, we rely on performance of a contract or steps requested by you, compliance with a legal obligation, and our legitimate interests in answering requests, securing the support channel, and establishing or defending legal claims, provided those interests do not override your rights. We rely on consent where applicable law requires it.

9 Storage Retention and Deletion

The analysis copy of a selected photograph is deleted immediately after the requested on-device analysis finishes. Temporary processing data is released when processing ends or the app session closes. Yard Wise does not retain a server copy because the app does not transmit the photograph or analysis to us.

Reports and app settings stored locally remain until you delete the relevant item, use an available clear-data control, delete the app, or erase the applicable device storage. Copies saved in Files, Photos, device backups, email, a print service, or another destination remain subject to the controls and retention rules of that destination. Deleting Yard Wise does not remove those external copies.

We retain support messages for up to 24 months after a request is resolved. We may keep limited records longer where a specific law requires it or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim. Such records are restricted to that purpose and deleted when the applicable period ends.

10 Security

Yard Wise uses the iOS app sandbox and available device data-protection features for information stored locally. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for support communications, including access controls and the transport protections provided by our email service. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Protect access to your device and review a report before exporting or sharing it. If a personal-data incident involving information we hold occurs, we will take appropriate containment and remedial measures and provide notifications required by applicable law.

You control camera, Photos, location, microphone, and notification permissions through iOS Settings. You can delete locally stored reports and app data through available in-app or iOS controls. Data that remains solely on your device must be accessed, exported, corrected, or deleted there because we do not hold it.

We may take proportionate steps to verify the request and will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to certain processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, appeal a decision, or complain to a data-protection authority. We do not discriminate against you for exercising rights provided by applicable law.
